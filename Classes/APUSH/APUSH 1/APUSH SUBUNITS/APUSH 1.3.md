#                     Columbian Exchange & Spanish Conquest  


- ## **Causes of trade**
	- ##### Now this increased trade was caused by a variety of things. One of was the improvements in ***Maritime Technology***, such as new ships like *caravels* with *lateen sails*. Or the compass and astrolabe which helped determine their location. Then Joint-Stock companies helped fund expeditions and get returns on their investments. Though even though they were funded by the people, they still relied on the state for safety and monopolies.
----

- ## **Effects of trade**
	- ##### The main thing that started the biggest effects was the ***Columbian Exchange***. The Columbian exchange refers to the *transfer of diseases, plants, animals, minerals, and people between the old and new worlds*. So lets talk about some things that had been exchanged
		- ##### Most notably was **diseases** that spread. Due to the old world being connected and trading between each other for millennia, they had built immunities to very deadly diseases. So when the Europeans interacted with the indigenous populations of the new world... well they got almost entirely wiped out. For example, the *Spanish introduced Smallpox which killed almost half of the indigenous population of Mexico and Central America.* They even brought measles and many more diseases, which led to the Native Americans dubbing these events *"The Great Dying*
		- ##### Another exchange was **Food & Plants** with major effects on the old and new worlds. *Europeans introduced Wheat, Olives, Grapes, Rice, and Sugar*. While *the Indigenous introduced Maize, Potatoes, and manioc* to name a few. In general, new food led to a more varied diet which led to more nutrition, as well as a **population BOOM in Europe**.
		- ##### Third we got **Animals**. Animals went both ways but the **horses** being introduced to the Americas allowed the Native Americans to have more efficient ways to hunt buffalos, and gave them advantages over tribes that did not have them. But also other animals like **sheeps, cattle, and pigs**.
		- ##### And lastly we got **Minerals**, which were mostly **Silver and Gold**. As the Conquistadors conquered The Inca and Aztecs they plundered their resources which made Spain super rich. For example, the *Potosi mountain* had so much silver they had slaves working there. But due to this it led to a *Price Revolution* where **inflation** started steadily for 150 years, as well as the start of the shift from **Feudalism to Capitalism**. **Feudalism** was an economic system that created a *closed system* focused on *land ownership*, while **Capitalism** was an open system focused on *manufacturing* and *trade*.
